# Roadmap

Qervon should grow in clear phases: foundation, domains, integrations, and expansion into adjacent logistics markets.

## Purpose

This roadmap keeps the platform goal focused while allowing future products to reuse the same architecture.

## Phases

- Foundation: kernel, module system, governance, and standards.
- Core logistics: dispatch, orders, couriers, tracking, fleet, billing, and notifications.
- Operational maturity: observability, resilience, security, and runbooks.
- Expansion: warehouse, field service, retail, medical logistics, and related verticals.
